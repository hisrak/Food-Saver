Food Saver is a digital platform that connects food donors with NGOs and volunteers to ensure surplus food reaches those who need it most.

Join Our Mission

Our Story

Food Saver was founded with a simple idea — that no good food should ever go to waste while people are hungry.

Every day, tons of edible food is wasted by restaurants, hotels, and businesses. At the same time, millions struggle to get a proper meal.

We built Food Saver to bridge this gap through technology, community, and compassion.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To reduce food waste and fight hunger by connecting food donors with NGOs and volunteers through a smart and efficient platform.

Our Vision

A world where no edible food is wasted and every person has access to safe and nutritious meals.
